# Product Steering
# Project: Standard Bank Delivery Squad Mobiliser
# Reference: #[[file:docs/requirements.md]]

## Mission
Empower Standard Bank delivery leads, chapter leads, and facilitators to rapidly mobilise high-performing cross-functional delivery squads for critical business initiatives.

## Target Audience
- Delivery Facilitators & Program Managers
- Engineering Chapter Leads & Resource Managers
- Practice Heads (Architecture, Data, QA, Cloud)

## Core Value Proposition
- Turn weeks of manual staffing negotiations into minutes of deterministic, transparent matching.
- Prevent delivery risk by surfacing capacity deficits and handover delays before project kick-off.
- Provide executive governance-ready proposals with verified match justifications.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints
- 100% Synthetic mock data for employee names, skills, and availability (strict POPIA compliance).
- Fully transparent, rules-based mathematical scoring (no black-box AI bias).
- Single-page fluid workspace with real-time feedback.
